Our motive

Straight from farmers, to your home

My Farm Grow is a delivery app that connects farmers directly with families. Your food comes from the people who grow it to your kitchen, with no middlemen in between.

Fair earnings for farmers

Selling directly means more of what you pay reaches the people who grow your food.

Fresher food for families

Fewer stops between the field and your door keeps every order fresh.

Honest sourcing

Every product is inspected before packing, so you can trust what reaches your home.

How it works

  1. 1

    Farmers harvest

    Local growers in Andhra Pradesh bring in their produce.

  2. 2

    We check and pack

    Each order is inspected and packed with care.

  3. 3

    Delivered to your home

    Your groceries arrive right at your door.
